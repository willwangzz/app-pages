Privacy Policy

SendEveryone

Overview

SendEveryone respects your privacy. The developer does not collect, receive or have access to any of your data.

Contacts Access

SendEveryone reads your contacts only so you can pick recipients. Contacts are read on your device and are never sent to the developer or any third party.

Groups and iCloud

When you send or save a group, the names, phone numbers and email addresses in it are saved in the app on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they also sync through your own private iCloud account so your groups appear on your other devices. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; the developer cannot see it. Deleting a group or the app removes it.

Message Sending

Messages and emails are composed and sent with Apple’s own Messages and Mail screens. SendEveryone does not read or store what you write.

Data Collection

SendEveryone does not collect any data. There are no analytics, tracking, ads or third-party SDKs.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email zongzheng_app@icloud.com.
