What it does

OdoSnap keeps a vehicle logbook for New Zealand (90-day) and Australia (12-week). Take a photo of the odometer, check the reading the app suggests, and save. Readings become trips, and Reports shows your business-use percentage and exports a PDF logbook or CSV.

Support

Questions, feedback or a reading the app got wrong? Email zongzheng_app@icloud.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

  • The reading is wrong — tap the number to fix it, or type it in. OdoSnap suggests a reading; you always confirm it before saving.
  • Restore my subscription — Settings › Subscription › Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with.
  • Cancel or change my plan — Settings › Subscription › Manage Subscription, or iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • My records on a new iPhone — sign in to the same iCloud account and turn on iCloud for OdoSnap. Records sync from your own iCloud; the first sync can take a few minutes.
  • Keep a copy — Settings › Data › Data management lets you export all records as CSV, or make a full backup file you can restore later.
  • Does OdoSnap tell me what I can claim? — No. It helps you keep the records; check with your accountant about what you can claim.

Privacy

Odometer photos and readings stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. No account, no sign-in. Anonymous recognition statistics are optional and off by default.

它能做什么

里程拍拍帮你记新西兰（90 天）和澳洲（12 周）的车辆日志。拍一下里程表，看一眼 App 读出来的数字，没问题就保存。读数会连成行程，「报告」里能看公务使用比例，并导出 PDF 日志或 CSV 表格。

支持

有问题、建议，或者遇到读错的情况，请发邮件到 zongzheng_app@icloud.com，最好写上你的 iPhone 型号和 iOS 版本。

常见问题

  • 读数不对 — 点一下数字就能改，也可以直接手动输入。里程拍拍只是给出建议读数，保存前都由你确认。
  • 恢复订阅 — 设置 › 订阅 › 恢复购买。要登录当初订阅用的 Apple 账户。
  • 取消或更换方案 — 设置 › 订阅 › 管理订阅，或者在 iPhone 的「设置 › 你的名字 › 订阅」里操作。
  • 换新 iPhone 后记录还在吗 — 登录同一个 iCloud 账户，并为里程拍拍打开 iCloud。记录从你自己的 iCloud 同步过来，第一次可能要等几分钟。
  • 想留一份备份 — 设置 › 数据 › 数据管理，可以把全部记录导出成 CSV，或者做一份完整备份，以后可以恢复。
  • 里程拍拍会告诉我能抵扣什么吗 — 不会。它只帮你把记录记好，能抵扣哪些请咨询你的会计。

隐私

里程表照片和读数只存在你的设备和你自己的 iCloud 里。不用注册，不用登录。匿名识别统计是可选的，默认关闭。
