PhonetiKit 正音

Fix Chinese contact sorting

让中文联系人按拼音正确排序

What it does

When your device language isn't Chinese, Chinese contacts lose their A–Z order and all end up under "#". PhonetiKit adds Mandarin pinyin to their phonetic name fields, so they sort correctly in Contacts, Phone and Messages across your Apple devices.

Features

  • Smart scan — finds Chinese contacts that are missing pinyin
  • Preview first — review every change before anything is written
  • Polyphonic characters — flags characters with more than one reading (曾, 单, 柏…) and suggests the right one
  • Phonetic audit — browse, search and edit pinyin you already have
  • Company names — handles contacts that only have an organization

Available for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Optimized for iPhone Duo.

它能做什么

当设备语言不是中文时，中文联系人会失去 A–Z 排序，全部挤到「#」下面。正音为这些联系人的注音字段添加普通话拼音，让它们在你所有 Apple 设备的通讯录、电话和信息里都能正确排序。

功能

  • 智能扫描 — 找出缺少拼音的中文联系人
  • 先预览再写入 — 每一项修改都可以先检查确认
  • 多音字识别 — 标出有多个读音的字（曾、单、柏……），并推荐正确读音
  • 审查拼音 — 浏览、搜索并修改已有的拼音
  • 公司联系人 — 支持只有公司名的联系人

支持 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac，已适配 iPhone Duo。
